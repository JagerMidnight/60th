Welcome to the 60th Winnipeg Scout Group

"We are the 60th, the mighty mighty 60th!"

We are a co-ed Scouting group that is focused on a youth-led, life-long approach to learning, we endeavour to emphasize a broad range of skills while striving to create an environment that is inclusive, respectful, and safe for everyone.

The 60th Winnipeg is divided into four levels:
  • Beavers (Ages 5-7)
  • Cubs (Ages 8-10)
  • Scouts (Ages 11-14)
  • Venturers (Ages 15-17)
Come join us on The Canadian Path!
